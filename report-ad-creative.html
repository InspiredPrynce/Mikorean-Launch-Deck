01 Creative Philosophy

Mikorean's ad creative should feel premium yet accessible — combining sleek product photography with relatable Nigerian contexts. Every ad should communicate quality, value, and local relevance.

Creative Principle

"Show the product in the hands of real Nigerians, in real situations, solving real problems." Avoid stock photography. Authenticity drives conversion.

02 Campaign Architectures

Architecture 01
Awareness — Brand Introduction
Hero video showcasing product range with Nigerian lifestyle context. 15s and 30s variants. Focus on brand story and quality positioning.
Architecture 02
Consideration — Product Deep-Dive
Product-specific ads with feature highlights, comparisons, and social proof. Carousel and video formats.
Architecture 03
Conversion — Direct Response
Urgency-driven ads with pricing, offers, and clear CTAs. Retargeting sequences for cart abandoners.
Architecture 04
Retention — Customer Advocacy
User-generated content, reviews, and community stories. Building long-term brand loyalty.

04 Visual Direction

  • Photography: Clean product shots on dark/grey backgrounds with dramatic lighting
  • Lifestyle: Real Nigerian settings — offices, homes, commutes, cafes
  • Color Palette: Dark backgrounds with electric blue (#00D4FF) accents
  • Typography: Bold, modern sans-serif. Large product names, minimal text
  • Motion: Smooth product reveals, subtle particle effects, clean transitions
  • User-Generated: Authentic customer photos with consistent filter/treatment
