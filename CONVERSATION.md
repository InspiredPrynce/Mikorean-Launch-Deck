## What Is This Project?

A comprehensive brand marketing strategy portfolio for **Mikorean**, a Chinese-inspired tech accessories brand entering the Nigerian market. The project produces 23 detailed strategy reports, a multi-page interactive HTML deck, and supporting documents — all prepared by **Henry Ofido, Brand and Marketing Strategist**.

## Market Context

- **Target Market:** Nigeria (Lagos primary, Abuja secondary, Port Harcourt tertiary)
- **Primary Competitor:** Oraimo (Transsion Holdings subsidiary) — #1 in Nigeria
- **Other Competitors:** Xiaomi Nigeria, Anker Nigeria, Baseus Nigeria, Romoss, Samsung Nigeria, Tecno Nigeria, Infinix Nigeria
- **Budget:** ₦17.5M first month, ₦6.4M ongoing monthly
- **Target Audience:** Gen Z (18-24), Millennials (25-35)

## Design System

- **HTML Deck Theme:** Dark mode with grey (#1a1a1a, #2d2d2d, #4a4a4a), White (#ffffff, #f5f5f5), Black (#000000, #111111), Electric Blue accent (#00D4FF)
- **Tech Stack:** Pure HTML/CSS/JS, Three.js (CDN) for hero particles, GSAP + ScrollTrigger (CDN) for scroll animations, Inter font (Google Fonts)
- **No frameworks** — all vanilla code
