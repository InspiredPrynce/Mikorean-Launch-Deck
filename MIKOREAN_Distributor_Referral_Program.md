# MIKOREAN — Distributor & Merchant Channel Strategy + Referral Program

**Prepared for:** Mikorean Brand Strategy Team
**Date:** August 25, 2026
**Scope:** Distribution channels, merchant network, and consumer referral engine for Nigerian market entry
**Budget Context:** ₦17.5M first month launch, ₦6.4M ongoing monthly
**Target Markets:** Lagos (primary), Abuja (secondary), Port Harcourt (tertiary)
**Prepared by:** Henry Ofido, Brand and Marketing Strategist

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nigeria's tech accessories distribution is fragmented across open markets (Computer Village Lagos, Onitsha Electronics Market, Wuse II Abuja), formal retail (Slot, SPAR), and online marketplaces (Jumia, Konga). Oraimo dominates with 200+ retail partnerships and deep penetration from Slot to street vendors. Mikorean needs a three-pronged distribution approach: formal retail partnerships for brand credibility, wholesale distribution for market coverage, and a consumer referral engine for organic growth. Target: 50 retail partners Month 1, 200 by Month 6, 500+ active ambassadors by Month 6.

### 1.2 Distributor Onboarding Program

**Mikorean Authorized Reseller (MAR) Certification**

- **Application:** Online form + business verification (CAC registration or shop photos)
- **Requirements:** Physical shop or verified online store, ₦200K minimum first order, branded display commitment
- **Benefits:** Wholesale pricing (30-45% margin), branded POS materials, co-op advertising fund, priority stock access, listed on Mikorean store locator
- **Training:** 2-hour online certification module covering product knowledge, durability messaging, warranty process, and upselling techniques

### 1.3 Retail Partnership Packages

### 1.4 Margin Structure

### 1.5 Inventory Management

- **Drop-shipping for online orders:** Jumia/Konga ship directly from Mikorean warehouse

- **Reorder system:** Automated alerts when stock hits 20% threshold
- **Seasonal planning:** Hand fans stock-up February-April; power banks year-round; smart glasses for festive season

## 3. CONSUMER REFERRAL PROGRAM — "MIKOREAN SQUAD"

### 3.1 Program Structure

- **Name:** MIKOREAN Squad — Refer & Earn
- **Tagline:** "Share the hustle. Earn the rewards."
- **Launch:** Month 2 (after initial brand awareness)
- **Platform:** Web dashboard + QR code on packaging

### 3.2 How It Works

1. Customer purchases any Mikorean product
2. Receives unique referral code (QR code on warranty card + digital via WhatsApp)
3. Shares code with friends/family via WhatsApp, social media, word of mouth
4. Friend uses code at checkout (online or in-store)
5. Both referrer and friend earn rewards instantly

### 3.3 Reward Structure

| Milestone | Referrer Reward | Friend Reward |
|-----------|----------------|---------------|
| First referral | ₦500 store credit | 10% off first order |
| 5th referral | Free cable (₦3,000 value) | 15% off |
| 10th referral | Free power bank (₦12,000 value) | 20% off |
| 25th referral | Free smart watch (₦25,000 value) | 25% off |
| 50th referral | VIP status + ₦50K cash reward | 30% off + free accessory |

### 3.4 Ambassador Tiers

**Bronze Ambassador (0-9 referrals)**
- 10% commission on each sale
- Access to exclusive WhatsApp group
- Monthly product previews
- Birthday reward (free accessory)

**Silver Ambassador (10-24 referrals)**
- 12% commission
- Free products for content creation (1 product/month)
- Featured on Mikorean social media
- Quarterly meetups with brand team

**Gold Ambassador (25-49 referrals)**
- 15% commission
- Free product line (any 3 products quarterly)
- Priority access to new launches
- Dedicated account manager
- Co-branded content opportunities

**Platinum Ambassador (50+ referrals)**
- 18% commission
- Full product line access (all 12 categories)
- Revenue share on campus/territory sales
- Annual retreat invitation
- Co-creation opportunities (product input, colorway design)
- Featured in Mikorean brand campaigns

### 3.5 Referral Tracking & Payouts

- Unique QR code per customer (printed on warranty card + digital)
- Real-time referral dashboard (web app at squad.mikorean.com)
- Commission tracking with daily updates
- Monthly payout (bank transfer or airtime)
- Leaderboard: Top 10 ambassadors featured on social media monthly
- Annual awards: Top ambassador wins ₦500K + full product line

### 3.6 Anti-Fraud Measures

- Minimum order value for referral credit: ₦3,000
- Referral credit expires after 90 days
- Maximum 50 referrals per account per month
- Duplicate IP/device detection
- Manual verification for Gold+ tier payouts
- One referral credit per unique new customer

## 7. RISK ASSESSMENT & MITIGATION

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Low retailer adoption | Medium | High | Competitive margins, free starter kits, co-op advertising |
| Referral fraud | Medium | Medium | Anti-fraud measures, manual verification for high tiers |
| Inventory stockouts | Medium | High | Safety stock policy, demand forecasting, fast reorder system |
| Distributor price undercutting | Low | High MAP policy, minimum advertised price enforcement | 
| Counterfeit products from unauthorized sellers | Medium | High | QR verification system, authorized reseller badge program |
| Cash flow from Net 30 terms | Medium | Medium | Limit Net 30 to Tier 1 only, Tier 2/3 cash-based |
