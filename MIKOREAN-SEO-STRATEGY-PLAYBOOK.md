## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Market Opportunity

Nigeria's mobile phone accessories market was USD $543.67M in 2025, projected to reach USD $1.0B by 2034 (7.06% CAGR). The earphones segment alone is growing at 6.24% CAGR. Power banks are treated as essential purchases — not luxury items — due to unreliable electricity supply. This creates a massive organic search opportunity where informational queries ("best power bank for NEPA blackouts") drive high-intent commercial traffic.

### 5.1 Core Web Vitals — Nigerian Mobile Priority

**Context:** 85%+ of Nigerian internet users access via mobile on 3G/4G connections. Speed optimization must prioritize low-bandwidth scenarios.

#### Target Metrics

| Metric | Target | Nigerian Reality | Action |
|--------|--------|-----------------|--------|
| LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) | <2.5s | Often 4-6s on 3G | Critical: Optimize hero images, implement CDN |
| INP (Interaction to Next Paint) | <200ms | Often 400ms+ | High: Reduce JavaScript, defer non-critical scripts |
| CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift) | <0.1 | Often 0.3+ | Medium: Set image dimensions, preload fonts |
| TTFB (Time to First Byte) | <800ms | Often 2s+ | High: Server optimization, CDN edge locations |
