01 Market Trends

Nigeria's tech accessories market is experiencing rapid growth driven by smartphone penetration, increasing disposable income, and growing tech-savvy population.

Key Trend

The Nigerian tech accessories market is growing at 40% YoY, faster than the global average of 15%. This presents a significant first-mover advantage for brands that establish early presence.

  • Smartphone Penetration: 85%+ and growing, driving demand for accessories
  • Audio Revolution: TWS earbuds growing 60% YoY in Nigeria
  • Power Solutions: Power banks and fast chargers essential due to power infrastructure
  • Premium-Value Shift: Consumers seeking premium features at value prices
  • E-commerce Growth: Online sales growing 45% YoY, especially for tech products
