02 Supply Chain

  • Sourcing: Direct from Chinese manufacturers with quality inspection protocols
  • Shipping: Sea freight for bulk (4-6 weeks), air freight for fast-moving SKUs
  • Warehousing: Lagos hub (primary), Abuja (secondary), regional fulfillment partners
  • Last-Mile: Partner with Kwik, Gokada, and traditional logistics for delivery
  • Inventory Management: Real-time tracking with safety stock at 30-day levels
