01 Tagline Options

10 tagline options tested for resonance with Nigerian tech consumers.

03 Content Pillars

  • Product Excellence: Features, specs, comparisons, demonstrations
  • Nigerian Lifestyle: How products fit into Nigerian daily life
  • Community & Culture: User stories, team culture, brand personality
  • Tech Education: Tips, tutorials, industry insights
  • Social Proof: Reviews, testimonials, user-generated content

04 Messaging Hierarchy

Three-layer messaging framework for consistent communication across channels.

  • Layer 1 — Brand Promise: "Premium tech accessories designed for Nigerian life"
  • Layer 2 — Value Proposition: "World-class quality at prices that make sense"
  • Layer 3 — Proof Points: Specific features, warranties, customer reviews
