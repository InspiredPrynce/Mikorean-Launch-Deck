## 2.3 Week 2: Skill Building

### Day 6 — Sales Methodology: The Mikorean Way

**The Mikorean Sales Process (7 Steps):**

```
Step 1: GREET → Step 2: DISCOVER → Step 3: MATCH → Step 4: DEMONSTRATE → Step 5: HANDLE → Step 6: CLOSE → Step 7: ADD
```

**Step 1: GREET (30 seconds)**
- Make eye contact, smile, approach with confidence
- Open with: "Welcome! Are you looking for something specific, or shall I show you something new?"
- Never open with "Can I help you?" (triggers "I'm just looking" response)

**Step 2: DISCOVER (2-3 minutes)**
- Ask 2-3 questions to understand the customer's need
- Key questions:
  - "What phone do you use?" (identifies cable compatibility)
  - "What's your biggest frustration with your current tech accessories?" (opens pain points)
  - "Are you buying for yourself or as a gift?" (determines value sensitivity)
- Listen more than you talk. Target: 70% listening, 30% talking

**Step 3: MATCH (1 minute)**
- Connect their need to a specific Mikorean product
- Use: "Based on what you've told me, I'd recommend [product] because [specific reason tied to their need]"
- Never lead with price — lead with value

**Step 4: DEMONSTRATE (2-3 minutes)**
- Put the product in their hands
- Show the feature that matters most to their specific need
- If they mentioned battery life, demonstrate the power bank capacity
- If they mentioned cable durability, let them bend and test it

**Step 5: HANDLE OBJECTIONS (variable)**
- Welcome objections — they mean the customer is interested
- See Section 4 for the complete objection handling framework
- Rule: Never argue. Acknowledge, then reframe.

**Step 6: CLOSE (1 minute)**
- Trial close: "Would you like to go with this one?"
- Assumptive close: "Shall I pack this for you?"
- Urgency close: "We have a limited stock at this location — this is the last one at this price"

**Step 7: ADD (1 minute)**
- "While I'm packing this, can I show you something that pairs perfectly?"
- Cross-sell: See Section 5 for product pairing strategies
- Target: Every transaction should have at least one add-on

### Day 7 — Discovery & Questioning Skills

**The SPIN Framework for Mikorean:**

| Phase | Question Type | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **S**ituation | Establish context | "What phone do you currently use?" |
| **P**roblem | Identify pain | "How often do your cables break?" |
| **I**mplication | Amplify pain | "When a cable breaks mid-charge, what happens to your day?" |
| **N**eed-Payoff | Solution visualization | "What if you had a cable that lasted 2+ years instead of 2 months?" |

**Practice Drill:** Pair up. One person is the customer, one is the salesperson. Customer has a "secret need" written on a card. Salesperson must discover the need in 5 questions or fewer. Rotate roles.

**Discovery Question Bank:**

*For Cables & Chargers:*
- "How many chargers/cables do you own that are broken or barely working?"
- "How long does your phone take to charge from 0 to 100%?"
- "Do you charge multiple devices at night?"

*For Power Banks:*
- "How often does your phone die during the day?"
- "Do you travel or commute long distances?"
- "Have you ever missed an important call because your phone was dead?"

*For Smart Watches/Glasses:*
- "Do you track your fitness or health data?"
- "How many hours a day do you spend in front of screens?"
- "Would you want a device that tracks your health without carrying your phone?"

*For Audio (Headsets/Earbuds):*
- "Do you work from home or in noisy environments?"
- "How important is sound quality for your calls or music?"
- "Do you prefer over-ear comfort or in-ear portability?"

*For Accessories:*
- "Has your phone ever fallen off a table or dashboard?"
- "Do you have neck or back pain from looking down at screens?"
- "How do you prop your phone when watching videos?"

### Day 9 — Upselling & Cross-Selling Techniques

**The Power of Product Pairing:**

| Primary Purchase | Recommended Add-On | Why It Works | Attachment Pitch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phone | Cable + Charger | Complete charging solution | "You're buying a new phone — let's make sure you have a fast charger that protects the battery." |
| Cable | Power Bank | Extended power access | "Great cable! Now you'll never run out of power on the go." |
| Smart Watch | Earbuds | Full tech ecosystem | "Your watch is connected — complete the setup with earbuds that work seamlessly with it." |
| Laptop Stand | Headsets | Complete WFH setup | "If you're working at a desk, you'll want quality audio for calls." |
| Phone Holder (Car) | Car Charger | Complete car setup | "Mount your phone AND charge it while you drive." |
| Earbuds | Phone Stand | Complete entertainment setup | "Enjoy your music while your phone is propped up perfectly." |
| Hand Fan | Power Bank | Extended outdoor use | "The fan charges with this power bank — take it everywhere." |

**The "While I'm Packing This" Technique:**
- Never ask "Would you like anything else?" (triggers "no")
- Instead say: "While I'm packaging this, let me show you one more thing that customers love with this product..."
- Physically bring the complementary product into view
- Explain the pairing benefit in 10 seconds

**Bundle Pricing Strategy:**
- Offer 10-15% discount on bundles (e.g., Cable + Charger + Power Bank)
- Create "Starter Kit" bundles: ₦15,000 for ₦20,000 value
- Create "Premium Kit" bundles: ₦35,000 for ₦50,000 value

### Day 10 — Communication & Customer Service Skills

**The Nigerian Customer Communication Guide:**

| Customer Type | Communication Style | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Professional (Corporate) | Formal, efficiency-focused | Lead with specs, certifications, business value |
| Student | Price-conscious, trend-aware | Lead with value, durability, social proof |
| Tech Enthusiast | Knowledgeable, comparison-driven | Lead with specs, compare to competitors honestly |
| Gift Buyer | Emotional, experience-focused | Lead with packaging, premium feel, gifting scenarios |
| Repeat Customer | Relationship-focused | Lead with familiarity, new arrivals, loyalty recognition |
| Window Shopper | Low-pressure, curious | Be helpful without pressure, plant seeds for return |

**WhatsApp Selling Best Practices:**
1. Respond within 2 hours (ideally 30 minutes)
2. Use voice notes for complex explanations (feels personal)
3. Send product photos/videos, not just text descriptions
4. Always include price in first message (transparency builds trust)
5. Follow up exactly once after no response (not twice — not pushy)
6. Use the customer's name in every message

**Handling Difficult Situations:**

*Angry Customer:*
- "I hear your frustration, and I'm sorry about this experience. Let me fix it right now."
- Never say: "Calm down" or "It's not our fault"
- Always: Own it, fix it, follow up

*Out of Stock:*
- "I'm sorry, we're temporarily out. Let me put you on the priority list and you'll be the first to know when it's back. Can I suggest an alternative in the meantime?"

*Warranty Claim:*
- "Let me take care of this for you. I'll process the claim right now and you'll have a resolution within [timeframe]."

## 7.4 Coaching Conversation Guide

### The O.A.S.I.S. Coaching Model

**O — Observe:** Describe what you saw, heard, or read
*"I noticed on your last three calls, you jumped to product features before asking discovery questions."*

**A — Ask:** Ask what the rep was thinking or feeling
*"What was going through your mind when the customer mentioned their budget?"*

**S — Suggest:** Offer an alternative approach (only after asking)
*"What if instead of jumping to features, you asked one follow-up question first? Like 'What matters most to you — speed, durability, or price?'"*

**I — Implement:** Have the rep practice the new approach right now
*"Let's role-play. I'll be the customer. Try the new approach."*

**S — Secure:** Confirm commitment and set follow-up
*"You committed to trying this on your next 5 calls. I'll review with you on [date]. What could get in the way?"*

---

### Coaching Do's and Don'ts

| DO | DON'T |
|---|---|
| Coach one behavior at a time | Try to fix everything at once |
| Be specific and behavioral | Say "do better" without specifics |
| Ask before telling | Give unsolicited advice first |
| Celebrate progress | Only focus on what's wrong |
| Follow up on commitments | Give feedback and forget about it |
| Coach the behavior, not the outcome | Criticize a good process that lost |
| Use real examples from their calls | Use hypothetical scenarios only |
| Make it a conversation | Make it a lecture |

## 10.2 The 7-Step Sales Process (Quick Reference)

```
1. GREET     → Warm, confident, eye contact, smile
              "Welcome! Looking for something specific, or shall I 
               show you what's new?"

2. DISCOVER  → Ask 2-3 questions, listen 70%, talk 30%
              "What phone do you use?"
              "What's your biggest frustration with your current accessories?"

3. MATCH     → Connect their need to a Mikorean product
              "Based on what you told me, I'd recommend [product] because [reason]"

4. DEMO      → Put product in their hands, show the key feature
              "Here — try this. Feel the quality. See how [feature] works."

5. HANDLE    → Welcome objections, A.C.E. method
              Acknowledge → Clarify → Evolve

6. CLOSE     → Clear ask, assume the sale
              "Shall I pack this for you?"
              "Would you prefer [option A] or [option B]?"

7. ADD       → "While I'm packing this, let me show you one more thing..."
              Cross-sell or bundle the complementary product
```

## 10.4 The F.A.B. Formula

```
F = FEATURE    → What it IS (specs, materials, technology)
A = ADVANTAGE  → What it DOES (compared to alternatives)
B = BENEFIT    → What it means for YOU (personal value)

Example:
F: "20,000mAh battery with dual USB output"
A: "Charges your phone 4 times over, or two phones at once"
B: "You leave home at 100% and stay charged all day — no battery anxiety"
```
