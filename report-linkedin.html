LinkedIn serves Mikorean's B2B opportunities — corporate gifting, bulk orders, and partnerships with Nigerian businesses. It also establishes thought leadership in the tech accessories space.

03 B2B Outreach

  • Target Companies: Tech startups, fintech companies, co-working spaces, corporate HR departments
  • Outreach Volume: 100 personalized connection requests per month
  • Follow-Up Sequence: 3-touch sequence over 14 days
  • Value Proposition: Corporate gifting solutions, bulk pricing, branded accessories
  • Meeting Cadence: 10 discovery calls per month, 5 proposals sent

04 Employee Advocacy

Empower team members to amplify Mikorean's LinkedIn presence.

  • Content Calendar: Weekly pre-written posts for team members to share
  • Training: Monthly LinkedIn optimization workshop for all employees
  • Incentives: Top advocate gets recognition and quarterly bonus
  • Guidelines: Brand voice guide for personal posts mentioning Mikorean
