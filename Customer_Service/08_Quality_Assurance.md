## 8.1 Call/Chat Scoring Criteria

### Scoring Rubric (100 Points Total)

| Category | Weight | Criteria | Points |
|----------|--------|----------|--------|
| **Greeting & Tone** | 15% | Warm greeting, professional tone, customer name used | 15 |
| **Active Listening** | 15% | Reflected back customer concern, didn't interrupt, asked clarifying questions | 15 |
| **Product Knowledge** | 15% | Accurate information, confident answers, knew product specs | 15 |
| **Problem Resolution** | 25% | Resolved issue completely, offered alternatives, took ownership | 25 |
| **Communication Clarity** | 10% | Clear language, no jargon, easy to understand | 10 |
| **Empathy & Rapport** | 10% | Acknowledged feelings, personalized interaction, genuine care | 10 |
| **Closing & Follow-Up** | 10% | Summarized resolution, confirmed understanding, warm close | 10 |

### Score Interpretation

| Score | Rating | Action |
|-------|--------|--------|
| 90–100 | Excellent | Recognize, share as best practice |
| 80–89 | Good | Minor coaching opportunities |
| 70–79 | Needs Improvement | Targeted training required |
| Below 70 | Unsatisfactory | Immediate coaching + follow-up review |

### Scoring Checklist (Per Interaction)

```
PRE-INTERACTION
[ ] Response within SLA target

[ ] Clear, concise language

[ ] Showed genuine concern

CLOSING (10 pts)

[ ] Confirmed customer satisfaction

TOTAL: ___/100
```

## 8.2 Customer Satisfaction Surveys

### Post-Interaction Survey (Sent via WhatsApp/Email)

**CSAT Survey:**

> Hi [Customer Name]! Thank you for contacting Mikorean support. We'd love to know how we did. Could you rate your experience?
>
> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (1–5 stars)
>
> 1 — Very Poor
> 2 — Poor
> 3 — Average
> 4 — Good
> 5 — Excellent

**Follow-up question (if < 4 stars):**

> We're sorry your experience wasn't perfect. Could you tell us what we could improve? Your feedback helps us serve you better.

**Follow-up question (if 4–5 stars):**

> Thank you! We're glad we could help. Would you mind sharing a quick testimonial we can use to improve our service?

### NPS Survey (Quarterly — Sent to Random Customer Sample)

> On a scale of 0–10, how likely are you to recommend Mikorean to a friend or colleague?
>
> 0–6: Detractor
> 7–8: Passive
> 9–10: Promoter

### Survey Distribution Rules

| Survey Type | Channel | Frequency | Sample Size |
|-------------|---------|-----------|-------------|
| CSAT | WhatsApp | Every resolved ticket | 100% |
| NPS | Email | Quarterly | 20% of active customers |
| Product feedback | Email | 7 days post-delivery | 30% of orders |
| Exit survey | All channels | On cancellation request | 100% |

### Survey Response Targets

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| CSAT survey response rate | > 30% |
| CSAT score (average) | > 4.2 / 5.0 |
| NPS score | > 50 |
| Survey completion rate | > 80% (those who start, finish) |

## 8.3 Mystery Shopping Program

### Program Overview

- **Frequency:** Monthly (2 mystery shops per month)
- **Channels tested:** WhatsApp, Instagram DM, Phone, Email
- **Scenarios tested:** Product inquiry, order issue, complaint, return request

### Mystery Shopping Scenarios

**Scenario 1 — Product Inquiry (WhatsApp)**
- Ask about a specific product's features
- Ask for a recommendation
- Test response time, knowledge, and tone
- Evaluate: speed, accuracy, helpfulness, closing

**Scenario 2 — Order Complaint (Phone)**
- Report a delayed delivery
- Express frustration
- Test de-escalation, empathy, and resolution
- Evaluate: tone, empathy, problem-solving, compensation offered

**Scenario 3 — Return Request (Email)**
- Request a return for a defective product
- Include a fake order number to test verification process
- Test process adherence and communication
- Evaluate: process knowledge, response quality, follow-up

**Scenario 4 — Product Comparison (Instagram DM)**
- Ask to compare two products
- Test product knowledge and recommendation skills
- Evaluate: knowledge, helpfulness, upsell opportunity

### Mystery Shopping Scoring

| Criteria | Weight |
|----------|--------|
| Response time (within SLA) | 20% |
| Tone and professionalism | 20% |
| Product knowledge | 20% |
| Problem resolution | 25% |
| Process adherence | 15% |

### Mystery Shopping Reports

- Delivered monthly to L3 Manager
- Individual agent scores shared with each agent
- Trends tracked quarterly
- Issues addressed in team training sessions

## 8.4 Training Schedule

### New Agent Onboarding (First 2 Weeks)

| Day | Topic | Duration |
|-----|-------|----------|
| 1 | Brand overview, values, and mission | 2 hours |
| 1 | Product range walkthrough (all 12 products) | 3 hours |
| 2 | CRM and ticket management system | 2 hours |
| 2 | WhatsApp Business, Instagram, Email tools | 2 hours |
| 3 | Script training and role-play exercises | 3 hours |
| 3 | Escalation procedures and matrix | 1 hour |
| 4 | Handling complaints and difficult customers | 3 hours |
| 4 | Marketplace management (Jumia/Konga) | 1 hour |
| 5 | Shadowing experienced agent (live interactions) | 4 hours |
| 6–10 | Supervised live interactions with coaching | 4 hours/day |
| 11–14 | Independent work with daily check-ins | Full days |

### Ongoing Training (Monthly)

| Session | Topic | Frequency |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| Product updates | New products, spec changes, pricing | Monthly |
| Soft skills | De-escalation, empathy, communication | Monthly |
| Process updates | Policy changes, new procedures | As needed |
| Market knowledge | Competitor analysis, market trends | Quarterly |
| Tool training | CRM updates, new features | As needed |

### Coaching Program

**Weekly 1-on-1 Coaching (30 minutes per agent):**
- Review 2–3 scored interactions
- Discuss areas of improvement
- Set goals for the week
- Address questions or challenges

**Monthly Team Training (2 hours):**
- Review common issues from the month
- Share best practices and success stories
- Practice new scenarios through role-play
- Update FAQ database based on new inquiries

### Training Materials

| Material | Format | Updated |
|----------|--------|---------|
| Product knowledge base | PDF + online wiki | Monthly |
| Script templates | Shared document | As needed |
| Escalation matrix | Printed + digital | Quarterly |
| FAQ database | CRM + website | Weekly |
| Training videos | Internal library | Quarterly |
| Role-play scenarios | Printed cards + digital | Monthly |
