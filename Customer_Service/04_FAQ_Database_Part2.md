## 3.6 CHARGERS

### Q1: What charger options does Mikorean offer?
**A:** Our charger range:
- **20W Type-C Fast Charger** — ₦5,500 (charges 0–50% in 30 min)
- **33W Super Fast Charger** — ₦8,000 (charges 0–60% in 30 min)
- **65W GaN Charger** — ₦12,000 (laptop + phone simultaneously)
- **Multi-Port Charging Station (4 ports)** — ₦10,000

All chargers have UK/Nigerian-compatible 3-pin plugs.

### Q2: Will the fast charger work with my phone?
**A:** Fast charging requires your phone to support it. Check:
- **iPhone 8 and later:** Supports fast charging (need USB-C to Lightning cable)
- **Samsung Galaxy S10 and later:** Supports 25W fast charging
- **Most Android phones 2020+:** Support 18W+ fast charging

If your phone doesn't support fast charging, the charger will still work at standard 5V speeds — it's safe and won't damage your device.

### Q3: Is it safe to use third-party chargers with my device?
**A:** Yes! Mikorean chargers are certified with CE, FCC, and over-voltage protection. They include:
- Over-current protection
- Over-temperature protection
- Short-circuit protection
- Automatic shut-off when device is fully charged

Using our charger won't void your device warranty.

### Q4: Can I charge my laptop with the 65W GaN charger?
**A:** Yes! The 65W GaN charger supports USB Power Delivery (PD), which can charge:
- MacBook Air / MacBook Pro 13"
- Dell XPS 13
- HP Spectre
- Most USB-C laptops

It also has a USB-A port for simultaneous phone charging. Check that your laptop charges via USB-C (not proprietary barrel connector).

### Q5: What's the difference between GaN and regular chargers?
**A:** GaN (Gallium Nitride) chargers are smaller, lighter, and more efficient than traditional silicon chargers. They produce less heat, charge faster, and take up less space in your bag. The 65W GaN charger is roughly 40% smaller than a standard 65W charger.

## 3.7 CAR CHARGERS

### Q1: What car chargers does Mikorean sell?
**A:** Our car charger range:
- **Mikorean Auto 20W** — ₦4,500 (1 USB-C + 1 USB-A)
- **Mikorean Auto 33W** — ₦6,500 (1 USB-C PD + 1 USB-A QC)
- **Mikorean Auto Dual 48W** — ₦8,000 (2 USB-C + 1 USB-A)

All feature LED power indicators and compact design.

### Q2: How do I install the car charger?
**A:** Simply:
1. Plug the charger into your car's cigarette lighter/12V socket
2. The LED indicator will light up blue
3. Connect your charging cable and device
4. That's it! No installation required

The charger fits standard 12V sockets found in all car models.

### Q3: Will the car charger drain my car battery?
**A:** No. Mikorean car chargers have built-in protection that cuts power when the engine is off. The charger only draws power when the car is running. If you leave a device plugged in after turning off the car, the charger will stop providing power after 30 minutes of inactivity.

### Q4: Can I charge two devices at once?
**A:** Yes! All our car chargers support simultaneous dual/triple device charging. The 33W model charges one device at full speed while the second charges at 12W. The 48W model distributes power intelligently across all three ports.

### Q5: Does the car charger support Android Quick Charge?
**A:** The **Auto 33W** and **Auto 48W** models support Qualcomm Quick Charge 3.0 on the USB-A port, and USB Power Delivery on the USB-C port. This means Samsung, Huawei, and other QC-compatible phones will charge at maximum speed.

## 3.8 HEADSETS

### Q1: What headsets does Mikorean offer?
**A:** Our headset range:
- **Mikorean Buds Pro** (over-ear wireless) — ₦12,000
- **Mikorean Buds Lite** (on-ear wireless) — ₦7,000
- **Mikorean Buds Sport** (neckband wireless) — ₦5,500
- **Mikorean Buds Classic** (wired with mic) — ₦2,500

All feature built-in microphones for calls.

### Q2: How long does the battery last on wireless headsets?
**A:** Battery life by model:
- **Buds Pro:** 30 hours playback, 40 hours with ANC off
- **Buds Lite:** 20 hours playback
- **Buds Sport:** 18 hours playback
- **Buds Classic:** N/A (wired — unlimited with device power)

Quick charge: 10 minutes charging = 2 hours playback (Pro and Lite).

### Q3: Do the headsets support noise cancellation?
**A:** The **Buds Pro** features Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) that reduces ambient noise by up to 28dB. The other models use passive noise isolation (ear cup seal). None of the models have transparency/ambient mode.

### Q4: Can I use the headset for gaming?
**A:** Yes! All wireless headsets have a low-latency gaming mode:
- **Buds Pro:** <40ms latency (best for competitive gaming)
- **Buds Lite:** <60ms latency
- **Buds Sport:** <80ms latency

Activate gaming mode by holding the multifunction button for 3 seconds. You'll hear "Gaming mode on."

### Q5: How do I pair the headset with multiple devices?
**A:** The Buds Pro supports dual-device pairing:
1. Pair with first device normally
2. Put headset in pairing mode (hold power button 5 seconds)
3. Pair with second device
4. The headset remembers both and auto-connects to whichever is available

Switch between devices by pausing on one and playing on the other.

## 3.9 EAR BUDS

### Q1: What ear buds does Mikorean offer?
**A:** Our ear buds range:
- **Mikorean Buds Air** (true wireless, stem design) — ₦8,500
- **Mikorean Buds Mini** (true wireless, compact) — ₦6,000
- **Mikorean Buds Sport** (wireless, ear hook) — ₦5,500
- **Mikorean Buds Basic** (wired, in-ear) — ₦2,000

### Q2: How do the Mikorean Buds Air compare to AirPods?
**A:** Our Buds Air offer similar features at a fraction of the cost:
- True wireless design with charging case
- Touch controls
- Built-in microphone
- 5-hour playtime (20 hours with case)
- Bluetooth 5.1

They don't have Apple's H1 chip (no automatic iOS switching), but they work excellently with both iPhone and Android.

### Q3: Are the ear buds sweatproof for exercise?
**A:** The **Buds Sport** (ear hook design) has IPX5 rating — sweat and splash proof, perfect for workouts. The **Buds Air** and **Buds Mini** are IPX4 (splash resistant), suitable for light exercise. The **Buds Basic** (wired) has no water resistance rating.

### Q4: How do I get the best fit for my ear buds?
**A:** Each pair comes with 3 silicone tip sizes (S, M, L). For best fit:
1. Start with the medium tips
2. Insert into ear and twist slightly to lock
3. If they feel loose, try the larger tips
4. If they feel tight or uncomfortable, try the smaller tips
5. A good seal = better sound quality and noise isolation

### Q5: Can I use just one ear bud at a time?
**A:** Yes! All Mikorean true wireless ear buds (Air, Mini, Sport) support mono mode. Just take one ear bud out of the case and use it alone. Perfect for staying aware of your surroundings while on a call.

### Q6: How do I clean my ear buds?
**A:**
1. Remove silicone tips and wash with mild soap and water (dry completely before reattaching)
2. Use a dry, soft brush to clean the speaker mesh
3. Wipe the body with a slightly damp cloth
4. Clean the charging case with a dry cloth
5. Never use harsh chemicals or submerge the ear buds in water
