# 7. ESCALATION MATRIX

---

## 7.1 Escalation Tiers

### Level 1 (L1) — Frontline Support Agent

**Authority:**
- Process standard returns (up to ₦10,000)
- Issue discounts up to 10% on next purchase
- Process replacements for items under ₦5,000
- Answer all product and order inquiries
- Process warranty claims for items under ₦10,000

**Handles:**
- Product questions and recommendations
- Order status and tracking
- Simple troubleshooting
- Standard complaints (first contact)
- Return/refund processing (within policy)
- Basic account inquiries

**Escalates to L2 when:**
- Issue cannot be resolved within 30 minutes
- Customer has contacted more than twice about the same issue
- Request requires authority beyond their level
- Technical issue requires specialist knowledge
- Customer is clearly frustrated and needs senior attention

---

### Level 2 (L2) — Senior Support Specialist

**Authority:**
- Process returns up to ₦25,000
- Issue discounts up to 20% on next purchase
- Process replacements for items up to ₦15,000
- Handle warranty claims up to ₦25,000
- Authorize goodwill gestures (free accessories, expedited shipping)

**Handles:**
- Complex technical issues
- Escalated complaints from L1
- Warranty claims requiring assessment
- Customer retention situations (cancellation threats)
- Social media escalation responses
- Cross-channel complaint coordination
- Marketplace dispute resolution

**Escalates to Manager when:**
- Issue cannot be resolved within 4 hours
- Customer threatens legal action
- Financial impact exceeds ₦25,000
- Safety concern reported
- Issue requires policy exception
- Customer has contacted 3+ times without resolution
- Social media post gaining significant traction

---

### Level 3 (L3) — Customer Service Manager

**Authority:**
- Unlimited return/refund authorization
- Unlimited discount authority
- Process replacements for any item
- Handle all warranty claims
- Authorize policy exceptions
- Approve compensation and goodwill gestures of any amount
- Make final decisions on disputed cases

**Handles:**
- All L1 and L2 escalations
- Legal threats and regulatory complaints
- Safety and product defect reports
- High-value customer (VIP) issues
- Systemic product quality issues
- Media inquiries and PR-related complaints
- Final authority on all customer disputes

---

## 7.2 Trigger Conditions for Escalation

### Immediate Escalation (Skip L1, go to L2 or L3)

| Trigger | Escalate To | Timeline |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Customer mentions lawyer/legal action | L3 (Manager) | Immediately |
| Product safety issue (injury, fire, electrical shock) | L3 (Manager) | Immediately |
| Social media post with >50 engagements | L3 (Manager) | Within 1 hour |
| Customer threatens suicide/self-harm | L3 (Manager) | Immediately |
| Government/regulatory complaint | L3 (Manager) | Immediately |
| Media journalist inquiry | L3 (Manager) | Immediately |

### Urgent Escalation (L1 → L2 within same interaction)

| Trigger | Escalate To | Timeline |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Customer has contacted 3+ times about same issue | L2 | Within 30 minutes |
| Issue cannot be resolved within 30 minutes | L2 | Within 30 minutes |
| Customer explicitly asks for supervisor | L2 | Immediately |
| Order significantly delayed (>7 days past promise) | L2 | Within 1 hour |
| Return/refund exceeds L1 authority (₦10,000+) | L2 | Immediately |
| Customer threatens to leave negative review publicly | L2 | Within 30 minutes |
| Technical issue requiring specialist knowledge | L2 | Within 1 hour |

### Standard Escalation (L2 → L3)

| Trigger | Escalate To | Timeline |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| Issue cannot be resolved within 4 hours | L3 | Within 4 hours |
| Financial impact exceeds ₦25,000 | L3 | Within 2 hours |
| Policy exception required | L3 | Within 2 hours |
| Customer has contacted 5+ times without resolution | L3 | Within 1 hour |
| VIP customer with unresolved complaint | L3 | Within 2 hours |
| Systemic product defect identified | L3 | Within 4 hours |

## 7.3 Resolution Timelines

| Escalation Level | Acknowledgment | Investigation | Resolution | Communication |
|-----------------|----------------|---------------|------------|---------------|
| L1 → Standard | Immediate | < 30 min | < 2 hours | Every 30 min |
| L2 → Urgent | < 15 min | < 1 hour | < 4 hours | Every hour |
| L3 → Critical | < 5 min | < 30 min | < 2 hours | Every 30 min |
| L3 → Legal/Safety | < 5 min | Immediate | Case-by-case | Continuous |

### Communication Requirements During Escalation

- **Customer must be informed** that their case is being escalated
- **Customer must be given** a reference/case number
- **Customer must be told** the expected resolution timeline
- **Customer must receive** updates at the promised intervals
- **Never leave a customer waiting** without communication

## 7.4 Compensation Guidelines

### L1 Compensation Authority

| Scenario | Compensation |
|----------|-------------|
| Minor inconvenience (delay < 3 days) | ₦1,000 discount on next order |
| Wrong item sent (resolved quickly) | Free accessory + ₦2,000 credit |
| Delayed delivery (3–5 days) | Free shipping on next order |
| Product not as described | Full return + free shipping |

### L2 Compensation Authority

| Scenario | Compensation |
|----------|-------------|
| Significant delay (> 5 days) | 15% discount + free accessory |
| Repeated issue (3+ contacts) | 20% discount + free product upgrade |
| Product defect (non-safety) | Free replacement + ₦3,000 credit |
| Negative experience overall | 20% discount + free accessory + personal follow-up |

### L3 Compensation Authority

| Scenario | Compensation |
|----------|-------------|
| Safety issue | Full refund + replacement + ₦10,000 credit + personal apology call from manager |
| Legal threat | Case-by-case (may include full refund + compensation + written apology) |
| Viral social media complaint | Full resolution + compensation + public response + personal outreach |
| VIP customer issue | Full refund/replacement + premium gift + personal follow-up |
| Product recall | Full refund + replacement + notification to all affected customers |

### Compensation Budget Rules

- L1 agents: Total compensation authority up to ₦10,000 per case
- L2 specialists: Total compensation authority up to ₦25,000 per case
- L3 manager: Unlimited, but must document any compensation > ₦25,000
- Monthly compensation budget review on the 1st of each month
- Any single compensation > ₦50,000 requires Director approval

## 7.6 Post-Escalation Follow-Up

### L1 Post-Escalation Review (Within 24 hours)

- Review how the case was handled
- Identify if the escalation was necessary
- Document lessons learned
- Update knowledge base if needed

### L2 Post-Escalation Review (Within 48 hours)

- Confirm customer satisfaction with resolution
- Review for systemic issues
- Update process if needed
- Report to L3 if pattern identified

### Monthly Escalation Report

- Total escalations by type and channel
- Resolution rates and timelines
- Compensation costs
- Recurring issues and root causes
- Process improvement recommendations
