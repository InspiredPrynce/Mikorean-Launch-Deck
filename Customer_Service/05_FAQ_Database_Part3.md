### Q1: What phone stands does Mikorean sell?

### Q2: Will the phone stand work with my phone case?
**A:** Yes! Our stands accommodate phones up to 12mm thick (including most cases). Otterbox and other ultra-thick cases may not fit the pocket stand — the desk and bed stands have wider cradles and work with virtually any case.

### Q3: What's the difference between the desk stand and bed stand?
**A:**
- **Desk Stand:** Sits on flat surfaces, adjustable angle (0–75°), compact and foldable. Perfect for video calls, watching videos at your desk, or FaceTiming while cooking.
- **Bed Stand:** Gooseneck arm with a clamp that attaches to headboard or desk edge. Flexible positioning — you can watch from bed, sofa, or even in the kitchen. Arm length: 60cm.

### Q4: Is the desk stand stable enough for video calls?
**A:** Absolutely. The desk stand has a weighted base (anti-slip silicone pad) that keeps it stable during video calls. It supports phones from 4" to 7" without wobbling. Even with typing near it, it stays firmly in place.

### Q5: Can I use the phone stand for gaming?
**A:** Yes! The desk stand holds your phone at a comfortable viewing angle for gaming. However, if you need to tap the screen heavily, we recommend the bed stand's gooseneck for a more stable, hands-free position. The pocket stand also works for propping up during casual gaming.

### Q2: Will the car vent holder fit my car?
**A:** Our vent holder fits 90% of car air vents — both horizontal and vertical slat styles. It uses a spring-loaded clip that grips the vent securely. It won't work with round vents or very thin vents. If unsure, send us a photo of your car vent on WhatsApp!

### Q3: Do I need a special phone case for the magnetic holder?
**A:** No special case needed! The magnetic holder comes with a thin metal plate that sticks to the back of your phone (or case). Simply:
1. Clean the back of your phone/case
2. Stick the metal plate on (adhesive backing)
3. Place your phone near the magnetic holder — it snaps in place

The plate is thin enough (0.5mm) to work with wireless charging on most phones.

### Q4: Is the bike holder secure enough for rough roads?
**A:** Yes! The bike/scooter holder uses a double-lock mechanism:
1. Spring-loaded clamp grips the handlebar
2. Phone cradle has rubberized grips and a security latch

It's tested on Lagos roads — it holds securely even on bumpy surfaces. However, we recommend using it at moderate speeds and checking the mount before every ride.

### Q5: What's included with the selfie stick/tripod?

- Outdoor events and parties
- Market trading
- Beach days
- Hot office environments

Fill the tank with clean water, press the mist button, and enjoy cooling relief.

- **Breeze Pro:** <45 dB (normal conversation level)
- **Breeze Max:** <50 dB (slightly louder due to mist motor)
- **Desk Fan:** <40 dB (quiet office environment)

None of our fans are disruptive in normal settings.
