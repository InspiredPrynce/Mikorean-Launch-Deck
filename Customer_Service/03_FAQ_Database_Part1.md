### Q2: Are Mikorean cables compatible with my device?
**A:** Check your device's charging port:
- **Type-C port** → Use our Type-C to Type-C or USB-A to Type-C
- **Lightning port** (iPhone 5 and newer) → Use our Type-C to Lightning
- **Micro-USB port** (older Android devices) → Use our Type-C to Micro-USB

### Q3: How durable are Mikorean cables?
**A:** Our cables feature double-braided nylon construction with reinforced stress points. They're tested to withstand 10,000+ bends. If your cable breaks within 6 months, it's covered under warranty.

### Q4: Can your 3-in-1 cable charge multiple devices at once?
**A:** Yes! The 3-in-1 cable has Type-C, Lightning, and Micro-USB connectors. You can charge all three simultaneously, though charging speed may be slightly slower when all three are in use. Total output: 5V/3A.

### Q5: Do your cables support fast charging?
**A:** Yes, our Type-C to Type-C and Type-C to Lightning cables support up to 60W power delivery for fast charging. Pair with a Mikorean fast charger for best results.

## 3.3 POWER BANKS

### Q1: What power bank capacity options do you have?
**A:** Our power bank range:
- **Mikorean Pocket 5,000mAh** — ₦6,000 (phone backup)
- **Mikorean Mid 10,000mAh** — ₦9,500 (1–2 full charges)
- **Mikorean Pro 20,000mAh** — ₦15,000 (3–4 full charges)
- **Mikorean Max 30,000mAh** — ₦22,000 (5–6 full charges)

All feature dual USB outputs and Type-C input.

### Q2: How long does it take to charge a Mikorean power bank?
**A:** Charging times (using a 20W adapter):
- 5,000mAh: ~1.5 hours
- 10,000mAh: ~3 hours
- 20,000mAh: ~5 hours
- 30,000mAh: ~7 hours

Using a standard 10W charger will take roughly 1.5x longer.

### Q3: Can I take a Mikorean power bank on a plane?
**A:** Yes! All our power banks are under 100Wh:
- 5,000mAh: ~18Wh
- 10,000mAh: ~37Wh
- 20,000mAh: ~74Wh
- 30,000mAh: ~99Wh

You can carry them in your hand luggage (not checked baggage). Airlines recommend keeping them in your carry-on.

### Q4: Does the power bank come with a cable?
**A:** Yes, all Mikorean power banks come with a Type-C charging cable. You'll need your own wall adapter (not included) — any standard USB adapter works.

### Q5: My power bank isn't charging. What should I do?
**A:** Try these steps:
1. Try a different cable and adapter
2. Press and hold the power button for 5 seconds to reset
3. Make sure the power bank isn't in sleep mode — double-tap the power button
4. If the LED indicators aren't showing, the power bank may need warranty service

If none of these work, WhatsApp us with your order number and we'll help!

## 3.4 SMART GLASSES

### Q1: What features do Mikorean Smart Glasses have?
**A:** Our Smart Glasses (₦18,000) include:
- Built-in Bluetooth speakers for calls and music
- Touch controls on the frame
- UV400 protection lenses (prescription-friendly)
- 8-hour battery life
- IPX4 splash resistance
- Voice assistant support (Siri/Google Assistant)

### Q2: Can I replace the lenses with prescription lenses?
**A:** Yes! The lenses are removable and can be replaced by any optician. We sell the frames with standard UV-protective lenses. Prescription lens replacement typically costs ₦3,000–₦8,000 at local optical shops.

### Q3: How do I pair the Smart Glasses with my phone?
**A:**
1. Turn on the glasses (hold power button for 3 seconds)
2. Open Bluetooth settings on your phone
3. Look for "Mikorean Smart Glasses" in available devices
4. Tap to pair — you'll hear a confirmation sound
5. You're connected! Music and calls will route through the glasses

### Q4: Are the Smart Glasses waterproof?
**A:** They're IPX4 rated, meaning they're splash-resistant. You can wear them in light rain or while sweating during workouts. They're NOT suitable for swimming or submersion.

### Q5: How do I control music on the Smart Glasses?
**A:** Touch controls on the right temple:
- **Tap once:** Play/pause
- **Tap twice:** Next track
- **Tap three times:** Previous track
- **Swipe forward:** Volume up
- **Swipe backward:** Volume down
- **Long press:** Activate voice assistant

## 3.5 SMART WATCHES

### Q1: What features does the Mikorean Smart Watch have?
**A:** Our Smart Watch (₦15,000) includes:
- 1.4" color touchscreen display
- Heart rate monitor
- Step counter and calorie tracker
- Sleep monitoring
- Message and call notifications
- 7-day battery life
- IP68 waterproof (swimmable)
- Compatible with iOS 10+ and Android 5+

### Q2: Is the Smart Watch compatible with my iPhone/Android?
**A:** Yes! The Mikorean Smart Watch works with:
- **iPhone:** iOS 10 or newer (iPhone 6S and later)
- **Android:** Android 5.0 or newer

Download the "Mikorean Fit" app from the App Store or Google Play to sync data and customize watch faces.

### Q3: How do I change the watch face?
**A:** Three ways:
1. **On the watch:** Long-press the home screen → swipe to browse faces → tap to select
2. **Via app:** Open Mikorean Fit → Watch Faces → choose from 50+ designs
3. **Custom face:** In the app, upload your own photo as a watch face background

### Q4: Can I swim with the Smart Watch?
**A:** Yes! The watch is IP68 rated, meaning it's waterproof up to 1.5 meters for 30 minutes. You can swim, shower, and wash hands while wearing it. Avoid hot water (saunas, hot tubs) as steam can penetrate seals.

### Q5: How do I charge the Smart Watch?
**A:** The watch uses magnetic charging:
1. Attach the magnetic charger to the back of the watch
2. Plug the USB end into any USB adapter
3. Full charge takes approximately 2 hours
4. You'll see a charging animation on the screen

Battery life: up to 7 days with normal use, 3–4 days with heavy GPS use.
