## 6.2 PRODUCT INQUIRY RESPONSES

### General Product Inquiry

> "Great question! Let me share the details on our [Product Name].
>
> **Price:** ₦[Price]
> **Key Features:** [2–3 features]
> **Available on:** Our website, Jumia, and Konga
>
> Would you like help choosing the best option for your needs, or do you have a specific question about this product?"

### Recommending a Product (Based on Need)

> "Based on what you've described, I'd recommend the [Product Name] for you. Here's why:
>
> - [Benefit 1 relevant to their need]
> - [Benefit 2 relevant to their need]
> - [Benefit 3 relevant to their need]
>
> It's priced at ₦[Price] and available on our website, Jumia, and Konga. Would you like me to share a direct link?"

### Price Comparison Between Products

> "Here's a quick comparison:
>
> **[Product A]** — ₦[Price]: [Feature 1], [Feature 2], [Feature 3]
> **[Product B]** — ₦[Price]: [Feature 1], [Feature 2], [Feature 3]
>
> [Product A] is better for [use case], while [Product B] is ideal for [use case]. Which one fits what you're looking for?"

### Out of Stock Response

> "I'm sorry, the [Product Name] is currently out of stock. Here's what I can do:
>
> 1. I can notify you as soon as it's back in stock
> 2. I can recommend a similar product: [Alternative Product] — ₦[Price]
> 3. You can check Jumia or Konga — they may have it available
>
> Which option works best for you?"

### Asking Clarifying Questions

> "I want to make sure I recommend the perfect product for you. A few quick questions:
>
> 1. What device(s) will you be using this with?
> 2. Is there a specific budget you have in mind?
> 3. Will you be using this at home, in the office, or on the go?"

## 6.4 RETURN / REFUND HANDLING

### Return Request Acknowledged

> "I understand you'd like to return your [Product Name] from order #[Order Number]. I'm sorry it didn't meet your expectations.
>
> **To process your return:**
> 1. Please share the reason for the return
> 2. Ensure the product is in its original packaging with all accessories
> 3. We'll arrange a pickup or provide a return address
>
> **Return window:** Within 7 days of delivery
> **Refund timeline:** 3–5 business days after we receive the item
>
> Can you tell me more about why you'd like to return it?"

### Return Approved

> "Your return request has been approved!
>
> **Order:** #[Order Number]
> **Item:** [Product Name]
> **Return method:** [Pickup / Drop-off at address]
> **Pickup date:** [Date] (if applicable)
>
> Please package the item in its original box with all accessories. Once we receive and inspect it, your refund of ₦[Amount] will be processed within 3–5 business days.
>
> Thank you for your patience!"

### Refund Processed

> "Good news! Your refund of ₦[Amount] for order #[Order Number] has been processed.
>
> **Refund method:** [Original payment method]
> **Timeline:** Depending on your bank, it may take 3–5 business days to appear in your account.
>
> If you don't see it after 5 business days, please reach out and I'll investigate further. Is there anything else I can help with?"

### Exchange Request

> "I'd be happy to help you exchange your [Product] for a different one.
>
> **Available for exchange:**
> - [Alternative Product 1] — ₦[Price]
> - [Alternative Product 2] — ₦[Price]
>
> If the new product costs more, you'll pay the difference. If it costs less, we'll refund the difference.
>
> Which option would you prefer?"

### Return Denied (Outside Window)

> "I'm sorry, but your return request for order #[Order Number] falls outside our 7-day return window (delivered on [Date]).
>
> However, here's what I can offer:
> 1. If the product has a defect, it's covered under our 6-month warranty — I can process a warranty claim
> 2. I can offer you a [X]% discount on your next purchase as a gesture of goodwill
>
> Which option works better for you?"

## 6.5 COMPLAINT RESOLUTION

### Acknowledging a Complaint

> "I'm really sorry to hear about this, [Customer Name]. That's definitely not the experience we want you to have with Mikorean.
>
> Your feedback matters to us, and I want to make this right for you. Can you help me understand exactly what happened so I can resolve this properly?"

### Product Defect Complaint

> "I'm sorry your [Product Name] isn't working as expected. That's not acceptable, and I want to fix this immediately.
>
> Here's what I can do:
> 1. **Warranty replacement:** We'll send you a brand new replacement at no cost
> 2. **Full refund:** If you prefer, we'll refund the full ₦[Amount]
>
> To proceed, I'll need:
> - A photo or video of the issue
> - Your order number
>
> Which option would you prefer?"

### Delivery Complaint

> "I completely understand your frustration, [Customer Name]. Waiting longer than expected for your order is disappointing, and I apologize.
>
> Here's what I'm doing right now:
> 1. I'm checking the live status of your delivery with [Carrier]
> 2. I'll call the delivery partner to expedite
> 3. I'll update you within [timeframe]
>
> If your order doesn't arrive by [new date], I'll [offer compensation — free accessory / discount / full refund]. You have my word on this."

### Billing/Pricing Complaint

> "I understand your concern about the charge. Let me look into this right away.
>
> Can you share:
> 1. Your order number
> 2. The amount you were charged
> 3. What you expected to pay
>
> I'll investigate and get back to you within [timeframe]. If there's an error on our end, I'll correct it immediately."

### Social Media Public Complaint

> (Public response on Instagram/Facebook)
> "Hi [Name], we're sorry to hear about your experience. This isn't up to our standards, and we want to make it right. Please DM us with your order details so we can resolve this immediately. We're here to help! 🙏"

## 6.6 ESCALATION MESSAGES

### Escalating to L2 Specialist

> "I want to make sure you get the best help for this, [Customer Name]. I'm connecting you with our specialist team who handles exactly this type of situation. I'll brief them on everything so you won't need to repeat yourself.
>
> Please hold for just a moment — I'll be right here with you."

### Escalating to Manager

> "[Customer Name], I want to ensure this is resolved at the highest level for you. I'm escalating this to my manager, [Manager Name], who has the authority to [specific action]. They'll be in touch with you within [timeframe].
>
> In the meantime, here's your reference number: [Case #]. You can use this for any follow-up."

### Warm Transfer Protocol (Agent to Agent)

> "Hi [Receiving Agent], I have [Customer Name] here regarding [brief issue summary]. Order number is [Number]. I've already [steps taken]. They need [specific help]. I'm transferring now — please confirm you have everything you need."
