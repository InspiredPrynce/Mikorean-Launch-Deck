## 2.3 Priority Levels

### Priority 1 — CRITICAL (Respond immediately)

- Customer threatens legal action
- Product safety issue (electrical fault, overheating, injury)
- Order contains wrong item for a VIP customer
- Social media viral complaint (>50 engagements)
- Bulk order issue (10+ units)

**Resolution target:** < 2 hours

### Priority 2 — URGENT (Respond within 30 minutes)

- Customer has repeated same issue more than once
- Order delivery significantly delayed (>7 days past promise)
- Warranty claim for expensive item (>₦15,000)
- Customer threatens to cancel/leave
- Multiple marketplace returns in short period

**Resolution target:** < 4 hours

### Priority 3 — NORMAL (Respond within SLA)

- Standard product inquiries
- Single order status checks
- General how-to questions
- Single return/refund requests
- Standard complaints

**Resolution target:** < 24 hours

### Priority 4 — LOW (Respond when available)

- Feature requests and suggestions
- General feedback
- Non-urgent partnership inquiries
- Social media follows/mentions without issue

**Resolution target:** < 48 hours

## 2.4 After-Hours Protocol

### WhatsApp & Instagram (After Hours)

- Auto-reply with next-business-day commitment
- Urgent flag system: orders placed in last 2 hours marked for first-priority morning response
- Any message received after hours gets tagged "After-Hours" for morning triage

### Email (24/7)

- Auto-acknowledgment within 5 minutes (24/7 automated)
- Full response next business day
- Emails received after 6 PM treated as next-business-day priority

### Phone (After Hours)

- Voicemail with callback commitment
- Emergency line for delivery-in-transit issues only: +234 XXX XXX XXXX

### Weekend/Sunday Protocol

- Reduced staffing (1 agent minimum)
- Sunday operations: 12 PM – 5 PM only
- Saturday: Full staffing recommended (highest volume day)

## 2.5 Peak Volume Management

### Identified Peak Periods

| Period | Volume Level | Primary Inquiry Type |
|--------|-------------|---------------------|
| Weekday mornings (8–10 AM) | High | Order inquiries, tracking |
| Weekday evenings (5–7 PM) | High | Post-work browsing, complaints |
| Saturdays (10 AM–2 PM) | Highest | All types |
| Flash sales/promotions | 3x normal | Orders, product questions |
| December holiday season | 4x normal | Gift orders, complaints |
| January (back to school) | 2x normal | Laptop stands, chargers, cables |
| Payday (25th–30th of month) | 1.5x normal | Orders, new purchases |

### Peak Period Staffing Rules

| Condition | Minimum Agents |
|-----------|---------------|
| Normal weekday | 1 L1 agent |
| Peak weekday hours | 2 L1 agents + 1 marketplace monitor |
| Saturday | 2 L1 agents + 1 L2 on standby |
| Flash sale / promotion | 3 L1 agents + 2 L2 on standby |
| December holiday season | Full team + temporary staff |

### Overflow Protocol

1. When queue exceeds 10 pending tickets, activate overflow
2. L2 agents assist with L1 tickets
3. Manager on-call for escalations
4. Auto-reply messages updated to reflect higher-than-usual volume
5. Extended hours authorized (up to 9 PM) during peak periods

---

## 2.6 SLA Breach Handling

### When an SLA Is About to Breach

1. **At 70% of SLA time:** Alert notification to assigned agent
2. **At 85% of SLA time:** Warning notification to agent + L1 lead
3. **At 100% (breach):** Auto-escalate to L1 lead for immediate action
4. **Post-breach:** Document root cause, update customer proactively

### Monthly SLA Review

- Review all breaches on the 1st of each month
- Identify patterns (product, channel, agent, time of day)
- Implement fixes for recurring breaches
- Update staffing or processes as needed
