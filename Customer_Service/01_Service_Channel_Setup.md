## 1.2 Instagram DM Workflow

### Account Setup

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Handle** | @mikorean.ng |
| **Bio** | "Mikorean Nigeria \| Chinese-inspired tech accessories \| Shop: mikorean.com \| Jumia & Konga" |
| **Highlights** | Products \| How To Order \| Warranty \| Reviews \| FAQ |
| **Link in Bio** | Linktree to website, Jumia store, Konga store |

### DM Response Protocol

1. **Auto-greeting** on first DM (via Meta Business Suite or ManyChat)
2. **Response tracking** in shared inbox
3. **Tagging system:** Product inquiry, Order issue, Warranty, Complaint, General
4. **Handoff to WhatsApp** for complex issues

### Instagram Handoff Message

> "Thanks for reaching out! For faster support with your order or technical question, message us directly on WhatsApp at +234 XXX XXX XXXX. We'll pick up right where we left off!"

### Instagram Story Integration

- **Weekly FAQ stories** addressing common questions
- **Product demo reels** linked to DM auto-replies
- **Order confirmation resharing** (customer posts unboxing, repost with permission)
- **Customer testimonial highlights** in Stories

### Instagram Comment Management

- **Respond to all product questions** within 2 hours (public comment)
- **Negative comments:** Acknowledge publicly, move to DMs for resolution
- **Positive comments:** Like and reply with thanks
- **Spam:** Hide and report

| Action | Timeline |
|--------|----------|
| Order confirmation | Within 2 hours of placement |
| Tracking upload | Within 24 hours of dispatch |
| Return processing | Within 48 hours of request |
| Negative review response | Within 24 hours (public + private) |

### Cross-Channel Deduplication Rules

- If a customer contacts via marketplace AND WhatsApp, unify the conversation
- Use customer phone number or email as unique identifier
- Never make a customer repeat themselves across channels
- Tag the ticket with all channels used

## 1.6 CRM & Ticket Management

### Recommended CRM Tools

| Tool | Use Case | Cost |
|------|----------|------|
| **HubSpot Free** | Email, basic ticketing | Free |
| **Tawk.to** | Live chat, phone | Free |
| **WhatsApp Business API** | WhatsApp automation | Varies |
| **Meta Business Suite** | Instagram DMs | Free |
| **Jumia/Konga Seller Centers** | Marketplace management | Included |

### Ticket Field Requirements

Every ticket must capture:

1. Customer name
2. Contact channel (WhatsApp/Instagram/Email/Phone/Marketplace)
3. Phone number
4. Email address
5. Order number (if applicable)
6. Product category
7. Inquiry type (inquiry/complaint/return/warranty/technical)
8. Priority (low/normal/urgent)
9. Assigned agent
10. Status (new/in-progress/awaiting-customer/resolved)
11. Resolution notes
12. Follow-up date (if applicable)
