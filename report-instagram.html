01 Feed Aesthetic

Mikorean's Instagram feed should communicate premium quality through consistent visual language. Dark backgrounds, dramatic product lighting, and a cohesive color palette create an instantly recognizable brand presence.

Visual Identity

Every post should feel like it belongs in a premium tech magazine. Consistent use of dark backgrounds (#111111), electric blue accents (#00D4FF), and clean typography creates visual cohesion.

  • Color Palette: Dark backgrounds, white text, blue accents — consistent across all posts
  • Photography Style: Dramatic lighting, product-focused, minimal props
  • Grid Pattern: Alternating product shots, lifestyle, and text-based content
  • Typography: Bold headlines, clean body text, consistent sizing

03 Instagram Shopping

  • Product Catalog: Full product range with optimized descriptions and pricing
  • Shoppable Posts: Tag products in every relevant post and Reel
  • Shopping Stories: Swipe-up links to product pages in all Stories
  • Live Shopping: Weekly Instagram Live sessions showcasing products
  • Product Launches: Use Instagram's product launch feature for new releases

04 Stories Framework

5-7 Stories daily to maintain top-of-mind presence.

  • Morning (8 AM): Product tip or feature highlight
  • Midday (12 PM): Behind-the-scenes or team content
  • Afternoon (3 PM): Poll, question, or interactive sticker
  • Evening (6 PM): Product showcase or customer story
  • Night (9 PM): Recap or next-day teaser
