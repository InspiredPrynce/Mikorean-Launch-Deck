01 Executive Summary

Nigeria's tech accessories market is won as much offline as online. Computer Village Lagos, Onitsha Electronics Market, and campus activations are where purchase decisions happen. Oraimo's 26-day nationwide roadshow and 7-month in-store activation across 9 cities demonstrate the power of offline presence.

Mikorean's offline strategy covers: market activations, university campus programs, corporate gifting, event sponsorships, roadshow campaigns, retail expansion, and community-level grassroots marketing. The "E Go Last" durability message must be proven through live demonstrations.

Nigerian Consumer Psychology

Nigerians trust people they see — not ads. The mechanic, the student rep, and the WhatsApp group admin are the real gatekeepers. Gifting a product to a respected community figure is worth 10x a paid ad. Live durability proof converts skeptics faster than any marketing campaign.

09 Grassroots Community Marketing

Barbershop & Salon Network

Barbershops are Nigeria's unofficial community centers. Men spend 1-2 hours weekly here.

  • Distribute 500 branded mirrors/posters to barbershops in Lagos
  • Free earbuds sample with every barbershop partnership
  • Barbers earn 15% commission on earbuds and hand fan sales
  • Monthly "Barber of the Month" feature on Mikorean social media

Church & Religious Organization Partnerships

  • Sponsor charging stations at church conferences (100K+ attendance)
  • Gift power banks to pastors and church leaders
  • December carol charging booths
  • Target: 20 churches Lagos, 10 Abuja, 5 PH

POS Agent Network

  • Partner with 200 POS agents across Lagos, Abuja, PH
  • 20% commission on referred sales
  • Branded POS terminal stickers
  • Free power bank for every 10 referrals

Okada / Keke NAPEP Branding

Okada and keke riders are mobile billboards reaching millions daily. Low cost, high visibility.

  • Branded stickers on 500 okada/keke in Lagos
  • "Mikorean — E Go Last" messaging
  • QR code for WhatsApp follow
  • Budget: ₦100K for stickers + ₦50K for rider incentives
