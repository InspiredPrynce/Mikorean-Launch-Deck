01 Executive Summary

Nigeria's tech accessories distribution is fragmented across open markets, formal retail, and online marketplaces. Oraimo dominates with 200+ retail partnerships. Mikorean needs a three-pronged approach: formal retail partnerships for credibility, wholesale distribution for coverage, and a consumer referral engine for organic growth.

Target

50 retail partners Month 1, 200 by Month 6, 500+ active ambassadors generating 25% of total sales by Month 6.

04 Consumer Referral Program — "MIKOREAN Squad"

Tagline: "Share the hustle. Earn the rewards." Launch Month 2 after initial brand awareness.

How It Works

  • Customer purchases any Mikorean product and receives unique referral code (QR on warranty card + digital)
  • Shares code with friends/family via WhatsApp, social media, word of mouth
  • Friend uses code at checkout (online or in-store)
  • Both referrer and friend earn rewards instantly

05 Ambassador Tiers

Bronze (0-9 referrals)

10% commission, WhatsApp group access, monthly product previews, birthday reward

Silver (10-24 referrals)

12% commission, free products for content creation, social media features, quarterly meetups

Gold (25-49 referrals)

15% commission, 3 free products quarterly, priority launch access, dedicated account manager, co-branded content

Platinum (50+ referrals)

18% commission, full product line access, revenue share, annual retreat, co-creation opportunities, brand campaign features

Anti-Fraud Measures

₦3,000 minimum order for credit, 90-day expiry, max 50 referrals/month, duplicate IP detection, manual verification for Gold+ payouts.

06 Hybrid Channel Integration

Online-to-Offline Flow

  • Customer discovers Mikorean on Instagram/TikTok/X
  • Checks product on website or WhatsApp catalog
  • Store locator shows nearest Slot / Computer Village / campus merchant
  • Purchases in-store or orders online with delivery
  • Joins referral program via QR code on packaging

Offline-to-Online Flow

  • Customer sees Mikorean at campus activation or market demo
  • Scans QR code to follow on Instagram/WhatsApp
  • Receives flash sale offer via WhatsApp broadcast
  • Purchases online with delivery
  • Shares referral code with friends
