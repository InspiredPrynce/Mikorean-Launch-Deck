## Description Variations (4 × 90 characters max)

| # | Description | Character Count |
|---|-------------|-----------------|
| 1 | Mikorean brings you premium cables, power banks, earbuds & more. Free delivery in Lagos & Abuja. Shop now at mikorean.ng | 89 |
| 2 | 12 tech accessories built for the Nigerian hustle. Bluetooth 5.3, 20000mAh, 36-month warranty. Affordable. Reliable. | 88 |
| 3 | Better specs than the big brands. Lower prices. Free delivery. Flash sale: up to 30% off. Shop Mikorean today. | 87 |
| 4 | Power banks, earbuds, smart watches & cables — all at prices that make sense. Free delivery. Order at mikorean.ng | 88 |

## Pin Strategy

- **Headline Pin 1:** Position 1 → "Mikorean — Power Your World" (Brand)
- **Headline Pin 2:** Position 2 → "Premium Tech Accessories" (Category)
- **Headline Pin 3:** Position 3 → "Free Delivery in Lagos" (Offer)
- **Description Pin 1:** Position 1 → Description #1 (Brand + Offer + CTA)

This ensures every ad impression leads with brand, category, and key offer — while the remaining 12 headlines and 3 descriptions rotate freely for variety and optimization.

## Ad Strength Optimization Checklist

- [ ] 15 headlines — diverse (brand, feature, offer, CTA, social proof)
- [ ] 4 descriptions — unique angles, no redundancy
- [ ] 3 pinned positions — brand, category, offer
- [ ] Keyword coverage — "power bank Nigeria", "cables Lagos", "earbuds Abuja"
- [ ] Character count compliance — all headlines ≤30, all descriptions ≤90
- [ ] Unique value props — no two headlines say the same thing
- [ ] CTA included — at least 2 headlines with explicit CTA language
- [ ] Price points mentioned — headlines 9, 12, 15
- [ ] Urgency elements — headline 15 ("Flash Sale Today")

## Winning Criteria

### Statistical Requirements
- **Minimum sample size:** 1,000 impressions per variation
- **Minimum runtime:** 7 days per test (to capture weekly behavior patterns)
- **Confidence level:** 90% minimum before declaring a winner
- **Statistical significance:** p-value < 0.10

### Metric Thresholds

| Metric | Winner Criteria | Loser Action |
|--------|----------------|--------------|
| **CTR** | +15% or higher vs control | Pause and replace |
| **CPC** | -15% or lower vs control | Scale winner |
| **Conversion Rate** | +10% or higher vs control | Implement winner |
| **CPA** | -20% or lower vs control | Scale winner |
| **ROAS** | +25% or higher vs control | Increase budget for winner |
| **View Rate (video)** | +20% or higher vs control | Use winning hook for all videos |

### Decision Framework

```
Test Complete (7+ days, 1000+ impressions)
    ↓
Statistically Significant? (p < 0.10)
    ↓ YES
Winner Identified? (meets threshold)
    ↓ YES
Scale Winner (increase budget 20-30%)
    ↓
Test Next Element
    ↓ NO
Extend Test (add 3-5 days)
    ↓
Still Inconclusive?
    ↓ YES
Kill Test. Move to next priority.
    ↓ NO
Winner Identified → Scale
```
