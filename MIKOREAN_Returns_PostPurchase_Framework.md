# 1. Return & Refund Policy

## 1.1 Policy Overview

**Return Window:** 30 calendar days from date of delivery (not date of purchase)
**Warranty Period:** 6–12 months depending on product category (see Section 5)
**Exchange Window:** 30 calendar days from delivery
**Refund Method:** Original payment method; store credit for no-receipt returns

### Nigerian Consumer Protection Compliance
This policy complies with:
- **Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act (FCCPA) 2018** — right to return defective goods
- **Nigerian Consumer Protection Council Act (Cap C25, LFN 2004)** — product quality guarantees
- **E-Commerce Guidelines (NCC)** — online purchase return rights
- **Sale of Goods Act (Cap S1, LFN 2004)** — implied condition of merchantable quality

> **Key Legal Requirement:** Under FCCPA, consumers have the right to return goods that are defective, not fit for purpose, or not as described — regardless of any store policy to the contrary. MIKOREAN policy meets or exceeds these statutory rights.

### Category A — Cables & Chargers (Standard)
| Condition | Eligible | Refund |
|-----------|----------|--------|
| Unopened, sealed packaging | ✅ Full return | 100% refund |
| Opened, unused, all accessories | ✅ Return accepted | 100% refund |

**Warranty:** 6 months — covers manufacturing defects, fraying, connector failure
**Notes:** Cables and chargers are high-volume, low-value. Processing cost often exceeds return value. Apply judgment: for items under ₦3,000, issue returnless refund with photo evidence.

## 1.3 Return Exclusions (Non-Returnable Items)

The following are **NOT eligible** for return:
1. Products purchased from unauthorized resellers (no proof of MIKOREAN purchase)
2. Products with serial numbers removed, altered, or unreadable
3. Products showing signs of unauthorized repair or modification
4. Products damaged by misuse, neglect, or accident (not manufacturing defect)
5. Free promotional items (no-refund on gifts with purchase)
6. Products with missing accessories that were included in original packaging
7. Items purchased more than 30 days ago (warranty claims still accepted for defects)

## 1.5 Exchange Policy

**Exchange Window:** 30 calendar days from delivery
**Exchange Conditions:**
- Same product, different color/size: **Free exchange** — price difference refunded or charged
- Different product of equal value: **Free exchange** — processed as return + new purchase
- Different product of higher value: **Price difference charged** at time of exchange
- Different product of lower value: **Difference refunded** to original payment method

**Exchange Limitations:**
- Exchanges are subject to stock availability
- If requested exchange item is out of stock, offer refund or store credit
- Marketplace orders (Jumia/Konga): Exchange handled through marketplace return + new order

# Appendix A: Regulatory Compliance Reference

## Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act (FCCPA) 2018

**Key Provisions Relevant to MIKOREAN Returns:**

1. **Section 114** — Consumers have the right to return goods that are defective, not fit for purpose, or not as described
2. **Section 115** — Goods must match their description and be of merchantable quality
3. **Section 116** — Sellers must provide refunds, replacements, or repairs for defective goods
4. **Section 129** — E-commerce transactions carry the same consumer protection rights as in-store purchases
5. **Section 133** — Consumers can file complaints with the FCCPC if dissatisfied with resolution

**MIKOREAN Compliance Measures:**
- 30-day return window exceeds minimum statutory requirements
- Warranty periods meet or exceed industry standards
- Refund processing within 7 business days exceeds FCCPC guidelines
- All return policies clearly communicated before purchase
- Customer service available via multiple channels
- Complaint escalation process documented and accessible
