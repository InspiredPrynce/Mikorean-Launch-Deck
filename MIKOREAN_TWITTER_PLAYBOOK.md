## 3. REAL-TIME ENGAGEMENT PROTOCOL

### Response Time Targets

| Interaction Type | Target Response Time | Priority Level |
|---|---|---|
| Customer complaint (public) | < 15 minutes | CRITICAL |
| Product question | < 30 minutes | HIGH |
| Mention/shoutout | < 1 hour | MEDIUM |
| DM inquiry | < 2 hours | HIGH |
| Negative review | < 30 minutes | CRITICAL |
| Influencer mention | < 30 minutes | HIGH |
| Trending topic opportunity | < 1 hour | MEDIUM |

### Engagement Categories and Playbooks

**CATEGORY A: Customer Complaints (PUBLIC)**

Detection triggers:
- "Mikorean" + negative keyword (bad, broken, waste, scam, fake)
- Direct @ mention with complaint
- Quote tweet with negative sentiment

Response Protocol:
Step 1: Acknowledge immediately (within 15 min)
Template: "We dey sorry to hear this, [name]. This no be the experience wey you deserve. Pls DM us your order details so we fit sort this out quickly. We go make am right. 💪"

Step 2: Take conversation to DM
Step 3: Resolve issue (replacement/refund)
Step 4: Follow up publicly (with permission)
Template: "Update: Issue resolved for @[name] — replacement dispatched. This na how we do things at Mikorean. Customer satisfaction na our priority. 🙏"

**CATEGORY B: Product Questions**

Detection triggers:
- "Does Mikorean have..."
- "Which [product] is best for..."
- "Is Mikorean better than..."
- Comparison questions

Response Protocol:
Step 1: Answer directly with value
Template: "Great question, [name]! For your use case, our [product] go work perfectly because [specific reason]. Check am out here: [link]. If you need more help, DM us! 😊"

Step 2: Link to relevant product
Step 3: Offer additional help via DM

**CATEGORY C: Trending Topic Participation**

Detection triggers:
- Nigerian tech trending topics
- Phone launch events
- Power outage complaints (NEPA/PHCN)
- Lagos traffic discussions
- Heat/weather complaints

Response Protocol:
Step 1: Assess relevance (Can we add value? Is it appropriate?)
Step 2: Draft response that adds value, not just promotes
Step 3: Post within 1 hour of trend emergence
Step 4: Monitor engagement and respond to replies

Template examples:
- NEPA topic: "NEPA don strike again? 🔋 We dey here for you. Mikorean Power Bank — full charge from start to finish. No wahala."
- Phone launch: "New iPhone don drop! 📱 You go need protection for that screen. Mikorean Phone Stand — stable, adjustable, affordable. Your new phone deserve better."
- Heat wave: "This heat dey show us pepe 😤🔥 Mikorean Hand Fan — 8 hours of cooling. Portable. Rechargeable. Survive this weather."

**CATEGORY D: Influencer/Creator Engagement**

Detection triggers:
- Tech influencers mentioning accessories
- Unboxing videos of competitor products
- Tech review accounts posting recommendations

Response Protocol:
Step 1: Engage authentically (like, retweet, reply with value)
Step 2: Do NOT pitch immediately
Step 3: Build relationship through consistent engagement
Step 4: After 3-5 genuine interactions, consider DM for collaboration

Template:
"Love this review, [name]! Your take on [topic] na exactly wetin we dey think too. Keep killing it! 💪"

**CATEGORY E: Competitor Mentions**

Detection triggers:
- Direct comparison tweets (Oraimo vs...)
- "Best accessories in Nigeria" conversations
- Alternative recommendation requests

Response Protocol:
Step 1: Never bash competitors directly
Step 2: Highlight Mikorean value proposition
Step 3: Let customers make the comparison

Template:
"We respect all brands wey dey hustle for this space 🙏 But for [specific feature], Mikorean dey stand out. 10,000+ bend tests. 14-day battery. Real ANC. Try am and see the difference yourself. 😏"

## 8. CRISIS RESPONSE MATRIX

### Scenario 1: Product Defect Report (Public Tweet)

**Detection:** Customer tweets about defective product with photo/video
**Response Time:** < 15 minutes

**Immediate Response (0-15 min):**
"We dey sorry to see this, [name]. This no be the standard wey we set for ourselves. Pls DM us your order details and we go sort this out immediately. We take quality seriously. 💪"

**Follow-up (15-60 min):**
- DM customer for details
- Process replacement/refund
- Document incident

**Public Resolution (within 24 hours):**
"Update: Issue resolved for @[name] — [replacement sent/refund processed]. We dey committed to quality and we go keep dey improve. Thank you for bringing this to our attention. 🙏"

### Scenario 2: Safety Concern (Product Safety Issue)

**Detection:** Reports of product causing damage (overheating, sparking, etc.)
**Response Time:** IMMEDIATE (< 5 minutes)

**Immediate Response:**
"We dey take this very seriously. Pls stop using the product immediately and DM us your details. We go investigate and provide replacement. Safety na our #1 priority. 🚨"

**Escalation:**
- Alert brand director immediately
- Contact product team for investigation
- Prepare official statement if needed
- Consider voluntary recall if widespread

**Official Statement (if needed):**
"Mikorean is aware of reports regarding [product]. We are conducting a thorough investigation. Customer safety is our top priority. All affected customers will receive immediate replacements. We will provide updates as our investigation progresses."

### Scenario 3: Negative Review Thread (Viral Complaint)

**Detection:** Thread gaining traction with negative sentiment
**Response Time:** < 30 minutes

**Strategy:**
1. Do NOT delete or hide (makes it worse)
2. Respond publicly with empathy
3. Take resolution to DM
4. Let satisfied customers defend organically

**Response Template:**
"We hear you, [name] and we dey sorry for this experience. This no be standard Mikorean quality. We want to make this right — pls DM us your order number. We go resolve am immediately and follow up personally. 🙏"

**If thread goes viral:**
- Issue formal statement
- Offer public resolution
- Share steps being taken to prevent recurrence
- Thank community for holding brands accountable

### Scenario 4: Competitor Attack (Direct or Indirect)

**Detection:** Competitor brand or fans attacking Mikorean
**Response Time:** < 1 hour

**Strategy:**
- Never attack back
- Take the high road
- Let product quality speak
- Engage supporters who defend organically

**Response Template (if needed):**
"We respect the hustle of every brand in this space 🙏 Competition make everybody better. We dey focused on giving Nigerians the best quality at the best price. That's our mission. 💪"

### Scenario 5: Misinformation About Brand

**Detection:** False claims about Mikorean (fake, scam, Chinese spy, etc.)
**Response Time:** < 2 hours

**Response Template:**
"We understand concerns about new brands. Here's the truth: Mikorean is a legitimate brand committed to quality. Our products are tested, certified, and backed by warranty. Check our reviews from real customers: [link]. We dey here to stay. 💪"

### Crisis Communication Principles

1. SPEED: Respond faster than the news cycle
2. EMPATHY: Acknowledge feelings before facts
3. TRANSPARENCY: Be open about what you know and don't know
4. ACCOUNTABILITY: Take responsibility, don't deflect
5. ACTION: Show what you're doing to fix it
6. FOLLOW-UP: Update until resolution is complete
7. LEARNING: Document and improve processes

### Internal Escalation Contacts

**Level 1:** Community Manager (all standard interactions)
**Level 2:** Marketing Lead (complaints, influencer issues, viral content)
**Level 3:** Brand Director + PR (crises, safety issues, legal matters)
