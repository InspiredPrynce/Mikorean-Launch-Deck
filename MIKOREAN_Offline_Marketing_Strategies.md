# MIKOREAN — Offline Marketing Strategies

**Prepared for:** Mikorean Brand Strategy Team
**Date:** August 25, 2026
**Scope:** Comprehensive offline activation playbook for Nigerian market entry
**Budget Context:** ₦17.5M first month launch, ₦6.4M ongoing monthly
**Target Markets:** Lagos (primary), Abuja (secondary), Port Harcourt (tertiary)
**Prepared by:** Henry Ofido, Brand and Marketing Strategist

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Nigeria's tech accessories market is won as much offline as online. Computer Village Lagos, Onitsha Electronics Market, and campus activations are where purchase decisions happen. Oraimo's 26-day nationwide roadshow and 7-month in-store activation across 9 cities demonstrate the power of offline presence. Mikorean's offline strategy must cover: market activations (Computer Village, Onitsha, Wuse II), university campus programs, corporate gifting, event sponsorships, roadshow campaigns, retail expansion, and community-level grassroots marketing. The "E Go Last" durability message must be proven through live demonstrations, not just claimed through advertising.

## 1. MARKET ACTIVATIONS

### 1.1 Computer Village Lagos — Anchor Market

**Why:** Computer Village is West Africa's largest electronics market. 500,000+ daily foot traffic. Every tech brand must have presence here.

**Activation Plan:**

| Activity | Frequency | Budget | Expected Reach |
|----------|-----------|--------|----------------|
| Product demo booth (high-traffic zone) | Weekly | ₦200K/event | 5,000+ visitors |
| "Mikorean Day" exclusive launch events | Monthly | ₦500K/event | 10,000+ visitors |
| Free product testing stations | Ongoing | ₦100K/week | 1,000+试用 |
| Durability challenge (drop test, stress test) | Weekly | ₦150K/event | 3,000+ views |
| Branded kiosk (permanent) | Ongoing | ₦2M setup + ₦200K/month | Continuous |

**Key Tactics:**
- Partner with 3-5 top dealers for co-branded displays
- Live durability demonstrations: drop tests, charge speed races, water resistance
- Free screen protector application with any purchase (foot traffic driver)
- QR code scavenger hunt: scan codes at different stalls for prizes
- Branded shopping bags distributed free (walking billboards)

**Dealer Partnership Structure:**
- 3-5 anchor dealers get exclusive territory rights
- 35% wholesale margin + 2% quarterly bonus
- Branded display stands provided free
- Monthly restocking visits
- Joint advertising in Computer Village WhatsApp groups

### 1.2 Onitsha Electronics Market — Eastern Nigeria Hub

**Why:** Second-largest electronics market in Nigeria. Gateway to South-East and South-South.

**Activation Plan:**

| Activity | Timeline | Budget | Target |
|----------|----------|--------|--------|
| Dealer onboarding (2-3 wholesalers) | Month 1 | ₦500K | 3 partners |
| Product launch event | Month 2 | ₦800K | 5,000+ visitors |
| Campus tie-in (UNIZIK, FUTO) | Month 2-3 | ₦300K | 2,000 students |
| Monthly demo events | Ongoing | ₦200K/event | 3,000+ per event |

**Key Tactics:**
- Igbo-language marketing materials (localization advantage)
- Partner with Onitsha market association for credibility
- Bundle deals for market traders (hand fans + power banks)
- Roadshow tie-in with Enugu, Aba, Owerri

### 1.3 Wuse II Abuja — Northern Nigeria Hub

**Why:** Abuja's tech market center. Government workers, professionals, and affluent consumers.

**Activation Plan:**

| Activity | Timeline | Budget | Target |
|----------|----------|--------|--------|
| Dealer onboarding (2-3 partners) | Month 1 | ₦500K | 3 partners |
| Corporate gifting pilot | Month 2 | ₦1M | 5 corporate clients |
| Mall activations (Shoprite, Novare) | Monthly | ₦300K/event | 5,000+ visitors |
| Professional networking events | Quarterly | ₦500K/event | 200+ professionals |

**Key Tactics:**
- Premium positioning (Abuja consumers pay more for perceived quality)
- Government/NGO bulk order focus
- Arabic/Hausa signage at market stalls
- Corporate bundle deals (power banks + cables for staff gifts)

## 2. UNIVERSITY CAMPUS ACTIVATIONS

### 2.1 Campus Activation Program — "MIKOREAN Campus Tour"

**Why:** 3M+ university students in target cities. Students are early adopters, brand loyalists, and social media amplifiers. Campus = tomorrow's market.

**Target Universities:**

| University | City | Student Pop. | Priority | Activation Type |
|-----------|------|-------------|----------|-----------------|
| UNILAG | Lagos | 40,000+ | High | Full activation |
| UNIBEN | Benin | 35,000+ | High | Full activation |
| FUTO | Owerri | 25,000+ | High | Full activation |
| UNIZIK | Awka | 30,000+ | Medium | Demo + sales |
| OAU | Ile-Ife | 25,000+ | Medium | Demo + sales |
| ABU | Zaria | 30,000+ | Medium | Demo + sales |
| UNILORIN | Ilorin | 25,000+ | Low | Pop-up |
| UNIMAID | Maiduguri | 20,000+ | Low | Pop-up |

### 2.2 Activation Playbook

**Pre-Activation (2 weeks before):**
- Partner with student union/tech club
- Social media teasers: "MIKOREAN is coming to [campus]"
- WhatsApp group seeding through campus ambassadors
- Poster campaign at hostels, faculties, cafeterias

**Activation Day Format:**

| Time | Activity | Purpose |
|------|----------|---------|
| 9:00 AM | Setup + branding | Visual presence |
| 10:00 AM | Free charging station opens | Foot traffic driver |
| 11:00 AM | Product demo sessions | Education |
| 12:00 PM | Durability challenge live | Proof of quality |
| 1:00 PM | Flash sale (campus-only pricing) | Conversion |
| 2:00 PM | Campus ambassador recruitment | Network building |
| 3:00 PM | Giveaway raffle (follow + share) | Social amplification |
| 4:00 PM | Pack-up + data collection | Lead capture |

**Campus-Specific Products:**
- Earbuds (study/music focus)
- Power banks (hostel power outages)
- Phone stands (online class setup)
- Cables (shared dorm charging)

**Campus Pricing:**
- 10% discount on all products at campus events
- Bundle: "Student Survival Kit" — cable + earbuds at ₦8,000 (₦10,000 value)
- Flash sale: First 20 buyers get free branded notebook

### 2.3 Campus Ambassador Program

**Recruitment:**
- Target: 10 ambassadors per university (80 total by Month 3)
- Requirements: Active social media, 500+ followers, tech-savvy
- Application: Online form + 60-second video pitch

**Ambassador Benefits:**
- 15% commission on sales through their unique code
- Free products for content creation (1 product/month)
- Featured on Mikorean social media
- LinkedIn recommendation letter
- Certificate of partnership
- Quarterly meetups with brand team

**Ambassador Responsibilities:**
- 3 social media posts/week about Mikorean products
- Organize 1 campus demo event per month
- Manage campus WhatsApp group (product info, deals)
- Collect customer feedback and product requests
- Report competitor activity on campus

## 8. GRASSROOTS COMMUNITY MARKETING

### 8.1 Barbershop & Salon Network

**Why:** Barbershops are Nigeria's unofficial community centers. Men spend 1-2 hours weekly here. Trust is built through conversation, not ads.

**Activation:**
- Distribute 500 branded mirrors/posters to barbershops in Lagos
- Free earbuds sample with every barbershop partnership
- Barbers earn 15% commission on earbuds and hand fan sales
- Monthly "Barber of the Month" feature on Mikorean social media

### 8.2 Church & Religious Organization Partnerships

**Why:** Churches are major distribution and gifting channels in Nigeria. December crusades, Easter programs, and church anniversaries = product placement opportunities.

**Activation:**
- Sponsor charging stations at church conferences (100K+ attendance)
- Gift power banks to pastors and church leaders
- Christmas carol charging booths (December)
- Easter conference product sampling
- Target: 20 churches in Lagos, 10 in Abuja, 5 in PH

### 8.3 POS Agent Network

**Why:** POS agents are everywhere in Nigeria. They interact with hundreds of customers daily. They need power banks, cables, and phone holders.

**Activation:**
- Partner with 200 POS agents across Lagos, Abuja, PH
- Offer 20% commission on referred sales
- Branded POS terminal stickers
- Free power bank for every 10 referrals
- Monthly POS agent meetup

### 8.4 Okada / Keke NAPEP Branding

**Why:** Okada and keke riders are mobile billboards reaching millions daily. Low cost, high visibility.

**Activation:**
- Branded stickers on 500 okada/keke in Lagos
- "Mikorean — E Go Last" messaging
- QR code for WhatsApp follow
- Budget: ₦100K for stickers + ₦50K for rider incentives

## 12. RISK ASSESSMENT & MITIGATION

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Low foot traffic at activations | Medium | High | Strategic location selection, social media amplification |
| Campus permit delays | Medium | Medium | Early engagement with student unions, backup dates |
| Mechanic/shop owner skepticism | Medium | Medium | Free product samples, commission-based model |
| Corporate sales cycle long | High | Medium | Start pipeline early, offer free samples, follow-up cadence |
| Roadshow logistics issues | Low | High | Pre-scout locations, backup vehicles, local coordinators |
| Weather disruption (rainy season) | Medium | Low | Indoor backup venues, tent setups |
