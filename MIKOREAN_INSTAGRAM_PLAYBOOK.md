## FEED AESTHETIC & GRID PLAN

### Visual Theme: "Tech Noir meets Lagos Vibrance"

**Primary Palette**:
- **Dominant**: Matte Black (#1A1A1A), Steel Grey (#4A4A4A), Pure White (#FFFFFF)
- **Accent**: Electric Blue (#00D4FF) - representing tech innovation
- **Secondary Accent**: Lagos Gold (#FFD700) - cultural warmth and local identity

**Mood**: Premium yet accessible. Think Apple Store aesthetics meets Lagos street energy. Clean, modern, but never cold.

### Grid Layout Strategy

**The 9-Post Grid Pattern** (Repeat every 9 posts):

```
Row 1: [Product Close-up] [Lifestyle Shot] [Quote/Text Graphic]
Row 2: [Behind-Scenes] [Product Flat Lay] [UGC Repost]
Row 3: [Tutorial/Tips] [Product in Use] [Promo/Announcement]
```

**Color Distribution Rule**:
- 60% of posts: Dark/moody backgrounds (black, grey)
- 25% of posts: Bright/lifestyle backgrounds (outdoor, indoor warm tones)
- 15% of posts: Text-heavy or graphic-based content

### Photography Style Guide

**Product Photography (40% of feed)**:
- **Background**: Matte black or white seamless paper
- **Lighting**: Soft directional lighting, slight shadow for depth
- **Angles**: 45-degree hero shot, straight-on detail shot, lifestyle context
- **Props**: Minimal - single tech accessory, occasionally coffee cup or notebook
- **Post-Processing**: High contrast, slightly desaturated except for product accent colors

**Flat Lay Templates**:

**Template 1: "Tech Essentials"**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│  [Power Bank]     [Cable]      │
│        [Phone Stand]           │
│  [Earbuds]       [Charger]     │
│        [Phone]                 │
└─────────────────────────────────┘
Background: Dark slate texture
Angle: Directly overhead
```

**Template 2: "Office Setup"**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│  [Laptop]                     │
│     [Tablet Stand]  [Watch]    │
│  [Headset]    [Cable]          │
│        [Phone Holder]          │
└─────────────────────────────────┘
Background: Wooden desk or marble
Angle: 30-degree elevated
```

**Template 3: "On-the-Go"**
```
┌─────────────────────────────────┐
│     [Backpack open]            │
│  [Hand Fan] [Power Bank]       │
│  [Earbuds]  [Cable] [Charger]  │
│     [Car Charger peeking]      │
└─────────────────────────────────]
Background: Concrete or urban surface
Angle: Slightly angled overhead
```

**Lifestyle Shot Guidelines**:
- Always feature Nigerian models (diverse, authentic)
- Locations: Lagos Island, Victoria Island, Lekki, Abuja landmarks
- Natural lighting preferred
- Show products in real-use scenarios
- Maintain 70% male / 30% female representation (matching target demo)

**UGC Reposting Criteria**:
- ✅ Good lighting and composition
- ✅ Product clearly visible
- ✅ Brand tag (@mikorean) included
- ✅ Positive sentiment
- ✅ No competitor products visible
- ❌ Blurry or low-quality images
- ❌ Controversial or inappropriate content
- ❌ Competitor branding visible

**Negative Feedback Response**:
1. Respond within 1 hour
2. Acknowledge the issue
3. Apologize sincerely
4. Offer solution
5. Take conversation to DM if needed
6. Follow up to ensure resolution
7. Document for future prevention

**Product Issue Response**:
1. Immediate acknowledgment
2. Apology and empathy
3. Replacement/refund offer
4. Expedited resolution
5. Follow-up satisfaction check
6. Learn and improve

**Tone**: Confident, approachable, tech-savvy, culturally aware

- Use Nigerian slang appropriately (e.g., "oba" for cool)

- Use excessive jargon
