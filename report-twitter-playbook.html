01 Executive Overview

This playbook provides Mikorean with a complete Twitter/X strategy for entering and scaling in the Nigerian tech accessories market. Twitter remains the primary platform for tech discourse, product launches, and community engagement in Nigeria's digital ecosystem.

The strategy focuses on building authentic community presence, driving product awareness through engagement-first content, and establishing Mikorean as the go-to brand for quality tech accessories among Nigerian tech enthusiasts.

Key Insight

Nigerian tech Twitter is one of the most active tech communities in Africa, with over 2.5 million active users discussing gadgets, reviews, and deals daily. Early presence here creates outsized brand awareness.

The 5 Hook Frameworks

  • The Controversial Take: "Unpopular opinion: [common belief] is wrong because [reason]. Here's why..."
  • The Challenge: "Reply with your setup and we'll rate it 1-10 📸"
  • The Thread Hook: "We spent ₦50M on research. Here are 7 things we learned 🧵"
  • The Nostalgia Play: "Remember when [old tech]? What would you tell your younger self?"
  • The Community flex: "Shoutout to everyone running [product] in their setup 🔥"

05 Community Building

Building a loyal community on Twitter requires consistent engagement, value-driven content, and genuine interaction.

Product Content — 32%
Community Engagement — 24%
Industry Commentary — 19%
Behind the Scenes — 25%

Community Growth Tactics

  • Reply Strategy: Respond to every mention within 2 hours. Prioritize tech conversations.
  • Spaces Hosting: Weekly Twitter Spaces on tech topics — invite Nigerian tech influencers.
  • RT & Quote Tweets: Amplify user-generated content and customer testimonials daily.
  • DM Outreach: Personal welcome messages to new followers with exclusive discount codes.
  • Hashtag Communities: Actively participate in #TechTwitter, #NaijaTech, #GadgetReview.
