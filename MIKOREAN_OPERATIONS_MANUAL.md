## 3. SUPPLY CHAIN & LOGISTICS

### Supplier Management
- **Source:** Chinese manufacturers (direct sourcing)
- **Lead Time:** 45-60 days sea freight
- **Minimum Order:** Per manufacturer (typically 500-1000 units)
- **Quality Control:** Pre-shipment inspection required
- **Payment Terms:** 30% deposit, 70% before shipping (negotiate to Net-30 after relationship established)

### Inventory Management
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|

| Stockout Rate | < 5% |

## 4. ORDER MANAGEMENT PROCESS

### Order Flow
1. **Order Received** (Website/Jumia/Konga/WhatsApp)
2. **Payment Confirmed** (Paystack/Flutterwave/Manual)
3. **Order Picked & Packed** (< 12 hours from confirmation)
4. **Quality Check** (product condition, packaging, accessories)
5. **Dispatched** (tracking number sent to customer)
6. **In Transit** (real-time tracking updates)
7. **Delivered** (delivery confirmation + review request)
8. **Post-Purchase** (Day 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 touchpoints)

### Order Processing SLA
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Order Processing Time | < 12 hours |
| Dispatch Cut-off Time | 2:00 PM WAT (same-day) |
| Tracking Notification | Within 2 hours of dispatch |
| Delivery Confirmation | Automated (Day 0) |

## 10. RISK MANAGEMENT

### Operational Risks
| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Stockout | Lost sales, customer frustration | Safety stock (20% buffer), reorder alerts |
| Delivery failure | Customer dissatisfaction, returns | Multiple courier partners, COD option |
| Payment gateway outage | Lost transactions | Backup gateway (Flutterwave), manual payment option |
| Quality defect | Returns, reputation damage | Pre-shipment QC, 12-month warranty |
| Currency fluctuation | Margin compression | Price reviews quarterly, forward contracts |
| Supplier delay | Stock gaps | 2-supplier backup per category |
| Negative viral moment | Brand damage | Crisis response protocol, monitoring |
